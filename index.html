Why Savanna Futures Matter

Savannas are living landscapes where people grow food, sustain livelihoods, exchange knowledge, protect biodiversity and build their futures.

Climate, land use, health and governance change these relationships together. SAFI studies the connections so decisions in one area can account for consequences in others.

Our Strategic Priorities

Connecting research, indigenous knowledge, innovation and policy to advance resilient landscapes, healthy populations, peaceful societies and inclusive development. These four institutional priorities complement SAFI’s five Research and Innovation Centres. Indigenous knowledge, culture, policy engagement and community participation remain cross-cutting across the Institute’s work.

Who We Are

The Savannah Futures Institute (SAFI) is a Ghana-based, African-led organisation connecting research with communities and decision-makers across Africa's savanna regions.

From Accra, we partner with universities, governments, traditional institutions, civil society, businesses and international collaborators to shape resilient futures.
