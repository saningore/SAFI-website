Why savanna futures matter

Why Africa's savannas matter

Savannas are living landscapes where people grow food, sustain livelihoods, exchange knowledge, protect biodiversity and build their futures.

Grasslands, woodlands, rivers and wetlands connect people with nature, culture, health and economic opportunity. Changes in climate, land use, health and governance affect these relationships together. SAFI studies these connections so that decisions in one area can account for their consequences in others.

Who we are

Who we are

The Savannah Futures Institute (SAFI) is a Ghana-based, African-led organisation working at the intersection of science, indigenous knowledge, innovation, policy and sustainable development.

SAFI connects knowledge about landscapes, health, food, peace, culture and economies to help communities and decision-makers shape resilient futures. From Accra, we build partnerships with universities, governments, traditional institutions, communities, civil society, businesses and international partners.
