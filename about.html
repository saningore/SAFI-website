About The Savannah Futures Institute

SAFI connects knowledge, people and action to shape resilient savanna futures.

The Savannah Futures Institute (SAFI) is a Ghana-based, African-led organisation working at the intersection of science, indigenous knowledge, innovation, policy and sustainable development. Our focus is the future of Africa's savanna regions and their connections with drylands, grasslands, wetlands, river basins and growing towns and cities.

We are developing an interdisciplinary institution that brings scientific research into dialogue with indigenous knowledge and practical experience. Our research agenda starts with African priorities and seeks partnerships that build lasting capability within African institutions and communities.

Our history

The Savannah Futures Institute (SAFI) is an independent research, innovation and strategic partnership institute dedicated to shaping resilient, prosperous and sustainable futures for the world's savanna regions. We work at the intersection of science, policy, enterprise and communities, generating evidence, developing innovative solutions and building partnerships that transform knowledge into lasting impact.

SAFI is the culmination of more than three decades of research, innovation, policy engagement, institutional leadership and community development across Africa and internationally. Rather than being a newly created organisation, the Institute represents the evolution of a long journey that began in the early 1990s through community-based sustainable agriculture, agroforestry, natural resource management and poverty reduction initiatives.

Over the past 35 years, this journey has expanded through the establishment of pioneering initiatives including the Kuka-Zuli Agroforestry and Sustainable Agriculture Project (KAFSAP), the Community Welfare Foundation (COMWELF), the Savannah Conservation Movement (SACOMO) and the African Centre for Sustainable Savanna Communities (ACeSSCo). It has also been strengthened through strategic partnerships with Ghana's leading research institutes, universities and government agencies, alongside international collaborations with universities and research organisations in the United Kingdom and beyond.

The experience underpinning SAFI includes leadership in interdisciplinary research, innovation management, knowledge exchange, institutional development, programme design and international partnership building. It is further supported by national and international recognition for excellence in agroforestry, community forestry, environmental sustainability and research leadership, together with extensive experience in securing, reviewing and managing competitive research and innovation programmes.

During that period, the founder and executive director led the organisations to win national and international awards and contributed to over $20 million in grant funding for strategic and innovative projects, linking science, policy and impact.

Today, SAFI brings together this accumulated knowledge within a single interdisciplinary platform focused on research, innovation, policy, investment and capacity development. The Institute works to accelerate transformative solutions that improve livelihoods, strengthen resilient economies, restore ecosystems and unlock the immense potential of savanna landscapes and their people.

Looking ahead, SAFI aims to become a globally recognised centre of excellence for savanna futures?connecting research with action, innovation with investment, and local knowledge with global solutions.

Why we exist

Despite their enormous ecological, cultural and economic significance, the world's savanna regions remain among the least served by integrated research and innovation systems. SAFI was established to fill this gap by bringing together multidisciplinary expertise, strategic partnerships and practical implementation to address complex challenges through systems-based solutions.

Our vision

Healthy landscapes, healthy people, peaceful societies, thriving cultures and food-secure economies across Africa's savannas, contributing knowledge and solutions for resilient societies worldwide.

Our mission

To generate and connect African-led research, data, innovation and indigenous knowledge; turn evidence into useful policy and investment options; strengthen people and institutions; and build equitable partnerships for resilient savanna futures.

Savannah Futures Academy

Strengthening people and institutions is central to that mission. The Savannah Futures Academy is being developed to connect research with practice through short courses, methods workshops, executive learning and fellowships for researchers, practitioners, policymakers and community knowledge experts. Learning activities will be announced with clear eligibility, costs, available support and application arrangements.

How we work

We frame research questions with the people who will use the findings. We bring together environmental, health, social, economic and cultural evidence to understand interacting risks. Where resources and partnerships allow, we will test responses through community-based research sites, living labs and policy dialogues, then assess what can be adapted elsewhere.

A living lab is a setting where researchers and users develop and test ideas together. A policy lab brings evidence and decision-makers together to explore workable choices. Observatories track change over time. These are planned delivery approaches, developed in stages as programmes become ready.

What guides us

  • Scientific integrity ? careful methods, honest interpretation and openness about uncertainty.
  • African leadership ? research questions and benefits grounded in African priorities.
  • Equitable partnership ? clear agreements on responsibilities, resources, data and recognition.
  • Respect for indigenous knowledge ? consent, attribution and appropriate control over access and use.

Where we work

Ghana is our institutional starting point. Our strategy is to build strong place-based programmes before extending comparative work across West Africa and other African savanna regions. International partnerships will connect this work with relevant experience in Australia, Asia and the Americas. These relationships are a direction for growth, not a claim of existing overseas offices.

Legal identity

The Savannah Futures Institute is registered in Ghana as a Company Limited by Guarantee and operates as a not-for-profit research and innovation organisation.
