SAFI Website: Editorial & Design Implementation Specification
Developer-ready revision brief | Version 1.0 | 10 October 2026
Website: https://safi-website-wheat.vercel.app/index.html
Purpose: supply approved-direction draft copy, page architecture, heading levels, design rules, content governance and an actionable implementation checklist. All proposed partnerships remain explicitly subject to formal approval.
1. Executive decisions
• Use one institutional name consistently: The Savannah Futures Institute (SAFI).
• Adopt a five-centre structure with one consistent name per centre across cards, menus, page titles and metadata.
• Retain two strategic programme priorities as “proposed/in development”, not signed partnerships or operational projects.
• Add visible People & Governance, Publications & Insights, and Partnerships routes or sections; publish only verified biographies, outputs and commitments.
• Apply a coherent H1–H3 hierarchy, responsive grid, accessible contrast, consistent card dimensions and meaningful calls to action.

3. Homepage: final draft copy and exact heading levels
Hero [H1]: The Savannah Futures Institute (SAFI)
Eyebrow: Ghana-based • African-led • Research • Innovation • Policy
Subheading: Research, innovation and policy for resilient savanna futures
Body: The Savannah Futures Institute (SAFI) connects interdisciplinary research, indigenous knowledge and strategic partnerships to address challenges facing Africa’s savanna landscapes and communities. Our work spans climate and ecosystems, population health, food systems, peace and security, and cultural knowledge—turning evidence into practical innovation, informed policy and sustainable development.
Primary CTA: Explore Our Research | Secondary CTA: Partner With SAFI
Section [H2]: Africa’s Savannas: Landscapes, People and Futures
Intro: Across Africa, savannas sustain food production, biodiversity, water resources, cultural heritage and regional trade. Yet climate variability, changing land use, health inequalities and insecurity are deeply interconnected. SAFI brings disciplines and communities together to develop responses that are grounded in evidence and local realities.
Image: authentic savanna landscape; descriptive alt text; credited and licensed.
Section [H2]: Our Vision for Resilient Societies
Intro: Our work advances four connected outcomes:
• [H3] Healthy Landscapes — Protecting ecosystems, water resources and productive land.
• [H3] Healthy People — Advancing population health, prevention and resilient health systems.
• [H3] Peaceful Societies — Supporting social cohesion, human security and constructive governance.
• [H3] Inclusive Futures — Strengthening food systems, indigenous knowledge and economic opportunity.
Section [H2]: Our Research and Innovation Centres
Intro: Five specialist centres connect disciplinary depth with collaborative research, innovation and policy engagement.
• [H3] Climate, Environment & Landscapes — Climate adaptation, biodiversity, wetlands, river basins and landscape resilience.
• [H3] Population Health Innovation Systems — Environmental health, health equity, prevention and resilient health systems.
• [H3] Peace, Security & Borderlands — Peacebuilding, conflict transformation, mobility, governance and human security.
• [H3] Indigenous Knowledge, Governance & Culture — Living knowledge systems, customary governance, language and cultural heritage.
• [H3] Food Futures & Economic Transformation — Food security, sustainable agriculture, bioeconomy, enterprise and inclusive livelihoods.
CTA: Explore All Centres. Note: align the research landing page, which currently uses different extended centre titles, to this naming convention.
Section [H2]: Strategic Programmes and Partnerships
Intro: SAFI is developing collaborative programmes that combine research, institutional capacity and practical pathways to impact.
[H3] Savanna Water, Indigenous Knowledge & Climate Resilience Initiative — A proposed flagship under discussion for a SAFI–Ghana National Commission for UNESCO strategic collaboration (2027–2030). Status label: Proposed collaboration; subject to agreement.
[H3] Bawku Municipal Assets, Investment & Regeneration Programme (BAIRP), 2026–2030 — An evidence-led programme concept focused on recovery, livelihoods, responsible investment and peace-sensitive regeneration in Bawku Municipality and selected conflict-affected neighbouring areas. Status label: Programme concept; stakeholder engagement ongoing.
CTA: View Programme Priorities
Section [H2]: Our Research-to-Impact Approach
Intro: SAFI combines scientific rigour, local knowledge and collaborative learning to support better decisions.
• [H3] Co-define the Challenge — Identify needs and priorities with stakeholders.
• [H3] Generate Evidence — Use interdisciplinary and context-sensitive methods.
• [H3] Co-design Responses — Bring policy, practice and community perspectives together.
• [H3] Test and Learn — Pilot innovations and assess feasibility and outcomes.
• [H3] Share and Scale Learning — Translate findings into policy, practice and future partnerships.
Section [H2]: People, Governance and Research Integrity
Suggested copy: SAFI is building an accountable institutional model guided by research integrity, inclusive governance and transparent partnerships. Meet the people contributing to our strategic direction and programme development.
CTA: People & Governance. Publish confirmed roles only; identify proposed appointments separately until accepted. Don’t put this on the website. Wait for biod and photos.
Section [H2]: Insights, Evidence and Policy Dialogue
Suggested copy: Explore research findings, policy perspectives, programme updates and opportunities for knowledge exchange. Display a “Coming soon” state until verified publications exist.
CTA: View Insights
Section [H2]: Collaborate With SAFI
Suggested copy: We welcome equitable collaborations with universities, research organisations, public institutions, communities, development partners and responsible investors. Contact us to discuss shared research questions, programme development and knowledge exchange.
CTA: Start a Partnership Conversation

5. Design system and alignment requirements
Element
Implementation specification
Palette
Forest #244B35; savanna #668C4A; earth gold #C3A25C; ivory #F6F3E9; charcoal #303B35
Typography
Inter or Source Sans 3; H1 44–52 px desktop, 32–38 px mobile; H2 30–36 px; H3 21–25 px; body 16–18 px
Container
Max width 1200 px; 24 px desktop side gutters; 16–20 px mobile
Spacing
8 px base rhythm; section vertical 64–88 px desktop, 40–56 px mobile
Cards
Equal height per row; consistent image ratio 4:3; heading and CTA aligned; 20–28 px internal padding
Buttons
One primary and one secondary style; consistent labels; visible keyboard focus
Images
Realistic licensed photography; avoid stock-like repetition; crop consistently; add alt text and captions when useful
Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA target; keyboard navigation; contrast testing; reduced motion; form labels
Headings
One H1 per page; H2 major sections; H3 subsections and cards; avoid jumping directly from H2 to H4
Responsive
Test at 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px; no horizontal overflow
