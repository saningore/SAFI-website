People and Governance

SAFI is building an accountable, African-led institute that connects rigorous research with the communities and institutions shaping savanna futures.

Who we are becoming

Knowledge, people and action

Our model combines governing-board oversight, executive leadership, centre specialists, fellows and programme partners. The People directory will introduce confirmed members of the Institute — with research interests, relevant experience and approved affiliations.

We work alongside farmers, herders, fisherfolk, health workers, customary authorities and youth innovators. The photographs below reflect the landscapes and communities our team serves — not decorative stock, but the living savanna system SAFI exists to understand and support.

Governance & research integrity

Board of Trustees

The Board provides strategic oversight, safeguards SAFI's mission and ensures accountability to partners and the public. Constituted membership, terms of reference and published biographies will appear here once formally approved.

Executive Leadership

Executive leadership translates the Institute's vision into programmes, partnerships and day-to-day operations. Confirmed executive appointments — including the Executive Director — will be listed in the directory below with approved photographs and short biographies.

Accountable leadership

We publish roles only when appointments are confirmed. Proposed advisers are not presented as appointed until acceptance and approval.

Centre Leadership

Each of SAFI's five research centres will be led by specialists who connect disciplinary depth with collaborative research and policy engagement:

  • Climate, Environment & Landscapes
  • Population Health Innovation Systems
  • Peace, Security & Borderlands
  • Indigenous Knowledge, Governance & Culture
  • Food Futures & Economic Transformation

Advisers and Fellows

SAFI's advisory network and fellowship programmes will bring in complementary expertise from universities, public institutions, civil society and customary governance. Associates will be listed with clear role descriptions once agreements and consent are in place.

Governance and Integrity

Research integrity, ethical review, knowledge rights and fair participation guide how we work with communities and partners. Read our institutional approach to governance, safeguarding and responsible data use.
