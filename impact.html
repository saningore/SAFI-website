Evidence of Change

SAFI measures impact through knowledge generated, capability strengthened, partnerships built, policies informed, innovations tested and community outcomes supported.

We use verified figures and documented case studies only. As programmes mature, this section will present completed work, current activities and lessons learned with clear status labels.

How We Understand Impact

  • Knowledge generated — research, policy briefs, working papers and open resources.
  • Capability strengthened — the Savannah Futures Academy, fellowships and institutional partnerships in Africa.
  • Partnerships built — equitable collaboration with universities, government and communities.
  • Policies informed — evidence used in dialogue, planning and decision support.
  • Innovations tested — living labs and community-based research with documented outcomes.
  • Community outcomes — livelihoods, health, peace and landscape resilience where verified.

Landscapes, Livestock and Livelihoods

Until verified programme case studies are published, these field themes illustrate the systems SAFI studies — mobility, biodiversity, nutrition and rural economies across savanna landscapes.

Cattle

Pastoral herds connect savanna landscapes, nutrition and rural livelihoods. Evidence on mobility, grazing and market access helps communities and policymakers shape resilient food systems.

Ostriches

Large savanna birds reflect healthy open habitats and biodiversity. Conservation and community stewardship along corridors link wildlife, land use and sustainable futures.

Goats

Small ruminants support household income and food security across drylands. Research and programmes track how herd health, fodder and value chains can strengthen local economies.
